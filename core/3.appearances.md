The Archons have two main appearances: the forms they take as apparitions or
projections, and the forms they take when forming and piloting mecha-golems.

# Curmiece

When in the form of an apparition or projection, Curmiece appears like a dark,
whispy man with wings attached to his feet and hands and horns attached to his
head. This form appears vague and shadowy, like smoke that never quite holds
onto a form.

The mecha-golems of Curmiece appear like a winged serpent.

# Venuze

When in the form of an apparition or projection, Venuze appears like a
voluptuous, scantily-clad woman surrounded by a floating red veil and swirling,
dancing swords.

The mecha-golems of Venuze appear like an 8-pointed star when viewed from the
front or back, and like a double-blade when viewed from the side.

# Maraze

When in the form of an apparition or projection, Maraze appears like a strong,
one-armed man in metallic armor. Electric sparks trace out intricate patterns
in his breastplate.

The mecha-golems of Maraze appear like an orb with an arrow sticking out of it.
This arrow is essentially the barrel/focuser of an antimatter beam weapon.
